Why this trip matters

A familiar calling in a new place.

For five years, our family called Mali, West Africa, home. Those years shaped how I see the world, how I love people, and how I think about sharing the hope of Jesus across cultures.

Our team from Fellowship Bible Church has been invited to come alongside VIA, a Fayetteville-based missions organization already deeply involved in Peru. VIA equips believers to reach communities where the Gospel is not widely known. Through their work, they've found that South American missionaries can sometimes be more readily welcomed in places like Egypt than Americans, creating an opportunity to mobilize Peruvian churches for ministry in Muslim communities in North Africa.

VIA has specifically asked our team to help train Peruvian believers preparing for ministry in Muslim-majority parts of Africa. My five years living and serving in Mali give me firsthand experience to share about Islam, relationships across cultures, and what it looks like to live and serve in that context. I'm coming to support VIA's established work, not to start something new.

VIA has also asked us to help provide a care retreat for pastors and their families serving in remote areas of Peru—a chance for rest, encouragement, and connection for people who spend their lives caring for others. Learn more about VIA ↗.

I'll also get to walk alongside young adults from Fellowship's Go Track as they experience cross-cultural ministry firsthand. I'm grateful for the opportunity to encourage the next generation of people preparing to go.

What we'll be doing

Two ministry locations. One purpose: to serve.

At VIA's invitation, we're supporting their established work in Peru through two specific needs: training future cross-cultural missionaries and providing a care retreat for pastors and families in remote communities.

01 / LIMA

Equipping future workers

VIA has asked for training to help prepare Peruvian believers for ministry in Muslim communities in North Africa. I'll share firsthand lessons about Islam, relationships, and cross-cultural ministry from five years living in Muslim-majority Mali.

02 / PUCALLPA

Encouraging pastors & families

VIA has asked our team to help provide a care retreat for pastors and their families serving in remote communities. We'll offer space for rest, fellowship, biblical encouragement, and conversations about marriage and ministry.

03 / ALONG THE RIVER

Supporting the whole family

We'll spend time with pastors' wives and children so ministry families can be refreshed together. I'll also mentor young adults from Fellowship's Go Track throughout the trip.

Where we'll spend much of our time

Life along the Ucayali.

Pucallpa is a gateway to Peru's Amazon region and communities along the river.

Many pastors and their families are scattered throughout remote parts of the region. We've been told that, because of where they live and serve, they rarely get a break or the chance to receive encouragement themselves. Our team is coming to listen, care for them, and create space for rest, connection, and renewed strength. Sometimes those who spend their lives serving others need to be served, too.

Meanwhile, back in Arkansas...

Trading Carpools for Canoes.

January is not exactly the quiet season at our house.

Jason is our church's Global Outreach Pastor and coaches varsity basketball at Rogers. Two of our boys play basketball, and Addy and Josiah have plenty going on, too.

So while I'm serving in Peru, Jason and the kids will be navigating basketball season, busy schedules, and a whole lot of carpools. I'm especially thankful for the friends and family who help make a trip like this possible. Please pray for my crew at home, too!

And no, the whole McMahan crew isn’t going this time—just me!
